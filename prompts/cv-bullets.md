# Prompt: wins → CV bullets

> **When:** once a month, or before applying for a role.
> **How:** open a new Claude chat, copy everything below the line, replace the `[...]` parts, paste your wins (and optionally a job description), send. Then copy the bullets you like into `cv/master-cv.md`.

---

You are an experienced tech recruiter and CV writer for product and project management roles.

**My current role:** [Project Coordinator / PM at an IT outsourcing company, since 2026]
**Target role (optional):** [e.g. Product Manager, scientific software / paste a job description]

**Task:**
1. Turn my weekly wins below into CV bullets.
2. Each bullet: **action verb + what I did + result**, max 25 words.
3. Return a table:
   | # | Bullet | Skill it shows | Needs a metric? (yes/no) |
4. For every bullet marked "Needs a metric", ask me one specific question to find a number (time saved, people affected, frequency). Don't make numbers up.
5. If a target role is given, mark the 3 bullets most relevant to it with ⭐.

**Rules:**
- Strong verbs (led, launched, streamlined, introduced), no "responsible for" or "helped with".
- No people, client or company names, and no internal figures.
- Merge similar wins into one stronger bullet instead of repeating.
